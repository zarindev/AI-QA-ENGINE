How CarePoint works

  • Receptionists register patients and book appointments with a doctor. A doctor cannot have two appointments at the same date and time.
  • Appointments go scheduled → checked in → completed. Scheduled appointments can be cancelled; cancelled appointments cannot be completed.
  • Completing a visit creates an invoice. The patient pays the consultation fee minus their insurance coverage: fee × (1 − coverage%).
  • Doctors write prescriptions for their own patients' visits. Duration must be at least 1 day.
  • Only administrators can add doctors and view financial reports. Deactivated doctors cannot be booked.
